    About Medicinal Co — Your Health Is What Matters to Us

    Medicinal Co (MCo) is a complete healthcare ecosystem at 250-B Commercial, Main Boulevard, Sector-C, opposite the Grand Jamia Masjid in Bahria Town, Lahore. We bring primary care, diagnostics, dentistry, specialist OPD clinics, and an in-house pharmacy under one roof.

    Our Direction

    Vision Statement

    To be the leading provider of ACCESSIBLE, COMPASSIONATE, and QUALITY healthcare solutions in Bahria Town Lahore, enabling families and communities to lead healthier and fulfilling lives.

    Smart Health for a Better Tomorrow

    Mission Statement

    At Medicinal Co, our mission is to deliver high-quality healthcare services that prioritize the well-being of our patients and the Bahria Town community. We are committed to:

    Affordability & Accessibility

    Ensuring that every family in Bahria Town and surrounding sectors has equitable access to dependable healthcare at a fair and transparent cost.

    Quality Care

    Providing comprehensive medical treatment, diagnostics, and pharmacy services while maintaining high standards of clinical excellence.

    Trust & Compassion

    Building strong patient-provider relationships based on empathy, clear plain-language explanations, trust, and respect.

    Advancing Medical Excellence

    Embracing modern diagnostics, on-site imaging, home care nursing, and continuous clinical improvement to deliver better outcomes.

    Supporting Healthy Communities

    Partnering with Bahria Town families and local initiatives to promote preventive screening, family wellness, and everyday primary care.

    How We Deliver

    Goal Statement

    Our overarching goal is to enhance the health and quality of life for our patients by prioritizing affordability, quality, and accessibility through preventive screening and treatment services.

    01.

    Affordable Healthcare

    Create cost-effective care pathways, keep everyday visits practical, and reduce unnecessary travel across Lahore.

    02.

    Quality Assurance

    Maintain strict clinical protocols, clean procedure spaces, and modern diagnostic equipment to ensure dependable care.

    03.

    Accessible Services

    Combine OPD clinics, X-ray, ultrasound, dentistry, pharmacy, and home nursing under one Main Boulevard address.

    04.

    Preventive Health Screenings

    Encourage routine check-ups and early diagnostic screenings to detect health issues early and support healthy lifestyles.

    05.

    Community Engagement

    Support health awareness for Bahria Town families and provide home-based nursing and imaging for patients who cannot travel.

    06.

    Continuous Improvement

    Regularly refine clinic workflows, appointment coordination, and specialist schedules based on patient feedback and community needs.
